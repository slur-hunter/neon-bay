NEON BAY

You are Rex, a courier in the sun-soaked city of Neon Bay. You owe $1,500 to Sal Vane, the loan shark who owns the docks. Take his jobs, pay the debt, and take the city back — on foot or behind the wheel of any car you can get your hands on. The cops will notice.

A complete open world in your browser: drive the streets, steal cars, dodge traffic and pedestrians, outrun the police, and finish all four jobs. Your HUD shows your money, health, wanted stars and a live minimap.
